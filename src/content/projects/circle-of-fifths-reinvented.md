## Problem

The circle of fifths is usually taught as a diagram to memorize, not a structure to understand — key signatures pile up as a list of facts rather than a pattern with any underlying logic.

## Approach

An interactive p5.js sketch, "Parity Patterns in the Circle of Fifths," visualizes the circle in a way that surfaces the parity structure underlying key signatures directly, rather than presenting them as a lookup table. A companion write-up, "A Procedural Method for Remembering Key Signatures," documents the reasoning as a repeatable procedure.

## Result

A working interactive sketch and an accompanying written method, both linked above.
