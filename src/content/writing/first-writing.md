## Begin with a question

[PLACEHOLDER] Open with a concrete question, observation, or problem. Give the reader enough context to understand why it matters without spending the first page announcing what the article will eventually do.

## Show the work

[PLACEHOLDER] Develop the idea using the evidence appropriate to it: a diagram, experiment, calculation, code sample, photograph, or carefully chosen example. Explain consequential decisions and omit routine detail.

## Leave a useful result

[PLACEHOLDER] End with what changed, what remains uncertain, or what should be tried next. A modest specific conclusion is more useful than a broad unsupported claim.
